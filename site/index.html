Start locally

  1. Download and extract the ZIP into a writable folder.
  2. Double-click START_EDGE_LAB.bat.
  3. Open 127.0.0.1:4178 when the console says the server is ready. Keep the console open.

This website provides downloads and documentation. The app runs on your computer.

What’s inside

  • Kraken triangular route estimates account for fees, available depth and order minimums.
  • Maker-first simulation models queue uncertainty, partial fills and delayed hedging.
  • Separate paper accounts retain positions, costs and realized results locally.

Before you trade

Automated strategies are paper-only. A separate Kraken live tab can submit manually confirmed real-money spot orders after credential setup. Paper results are simulations, not proof of executable profit.
